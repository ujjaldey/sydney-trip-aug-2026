## Blog Research

*Blogs from recent years (roughly 2022–2026) by photographers and nature lovers who avoid the crowds. Priority is given to landscape photographers and quiet-walks specialists.*

### 1. Gary P Hayes — Landscape Photographer (Blue Mountains local)
- **Blog:** [A Landscape Photographer's Guide to Australia's Blue Mountains](https://garyphayes.photography/a-landscape-photographers-guide-to-visiting-the-blue-mountains-australia/) + [Gary's Photo Hotspots](https://garyphayes.photography/garys-photo-guide/)
- **Why relevant:** Gary is a full-time landscape photographer who *lives* in Mount Victoria. His guide is written specifically for visiting photographers. Has a free 9MB PDF version of the guide — worth downloading.
- **Key takeaways:**
  - Breaks the upper mountains into 7 "photographic zones" and advises basing yourself near the light direction you want (west/east-facing lookouts are best for golden hour).
  - Best ~1–3 hr photographic trails: **National Pass loop** (Wentworth Falls, 5–8 waterfalls), **Leura Cascades loop**, **Furber Steps** (Katoomba), **Grand Canyon** (Blackheath), **Rigby Hill / Walls Lookout** (Bells Line, quiet).
  - Best sunrise/sunset spots: Govett's Leap sunrise (Blackheath), Narrow Neck sunrise (Katoomba), Hanging Rock (Blackheath), Hornes Point sunset (Mount Victoria), Mount Banks sunset.
  - Iconic shots: Sylvia Falls, Mount Solitary from Olympic Rock / Sublime Point, Three Sisters at sunrise from Eaglehawk Lookout.
  - Winter (Jun–Aug) = big rains and his best waterfall shots; dramatic skies at sunset, less at sunrise. Snow is rare but possible.
  - Drive from Sydney CBD ~90 min; car essential for photographers.

### 2. Walk My World — Travel Couple (hiking + lookouts)
- **Blog:** [28 Blue Mountains Lookouts including hidden gems](https://www.walkmyworld.com/posts/most-beautiful-lookouts-in-the-blue-mountains) (Nov 2022)
- **Why relevant:** A couple who hike constantly and actively seek out quiet, under-the-radar lookouts — very close to our style.
- **Key takeaways — quiet lookouts (rarely busy):**
  - **Fortress Rock, Leura** — rarely visited, view into Grose Valley, easy ~2.8km walk.
  - **Lockleys Pylon, Leura** — very quiet even midday; ~7km return; access road rough (4WD advised).
  - **Du Faur Head, Leura** — "hardly anyone knows about it"; effort to reach, worth it.
  - **Butterbox Point, Leura** — unknown spot, dramatic, ~3km return; end of Mount Hay Road (unsealed).
  - **Castle Head, Katoomba** — quiet, peaceful, views of Ruined Castle & Mount Solitary.
  - **Olympian Rock, Leura** — "not another soul in sight" on a Saturday midday.
  - **Rigby Hill & Walls Lookout, Bilpin/Mount Wilson** — rarely visited, unrailed, wild, great sunset.
  - **Kanangra Walls / Plateau** — solitude and phenomenal views (distant, needs ~2hr drive on rough road).
- **Key takeaways — accessible & iconic:**
  - **Lincoln's Rock, Wentworth Falls** — their favourite; 2-min walk; huge space even when busy; best at sunset.
  - **Echo Point, Katoomba** — go at sunrise (or at night — lit up, no crowds).
  - **Govetts Leap, Blackheath** — sunrise, only the odd photographer; busy later in day.
  - **Hargraves Lookout, Blackheath** — golden hour over Megalong Valley.
- **Accommodation notes:** Lilianfels (luxury, metres from Three Sisters, fine dining Darley's), Katoomba YHA (budget, private rooms). Full guide: [where to stay in the Blue Mountains](https://www.walkmyworld.com/posts/blue-mountains-accommodation-hotels).

### 3. NSW National Parks & Wildlife Service (official, updated Apr 2026)
- **Blog:** [Blue Mountains spots that give the must-sees a run for their money](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/get-inspired/stories/blue-mountains-spots-that-give-the-must-sees-a-run-for-their-money)
- **Why relevant:** Official source for quieter alternatives to the icons; park alerts & current conditions.
- **Key takeaways:**
  - **Sublime Point, Leura** — morning to catch Mount Solitary emerging from mist.
  - **Mount Hay Road, Leura** — epic vistas, "probably have it all to yourself", drive or mountain bike.
  - **National Pass + Overcliff–Undercliff Track, Wentworth Falls** — cliff-edge views, swimming holes; finish with coffee at Conservation Hut.
  - **Pulpit Rock lookout + Pulpit walking track from Govetts Leap, Blackheath** — isolated pinnacle, secret picnic, 280° views.
  - **Portal lookout & Blue Pool, Glenbrook** — swimming below epic green backdrop.
  - **Weather (early Aug):** cold — min ~2°C, max ~13–15°C. Always check [park alerts](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/visit-a-park/parks/blue-mountains-national-park/local-alerts).

### 4. My Katoomba — Local Cottage Hosts (Katoomba locals)
- **Blog:** [10 Hidden Walks in the Blue Mountains Most Tourists Never Find](https://www.mykatoomba.com/blog/hidden-walks-blue-mountains) (Feb 2026)
- **Why relevant:** Written by people who live and host in Katoomba; exactly the "quiet trails locals keep" angle.
- **Key takeaways:**
  - **Valley of the Waters Circuit, Wentworth Falls** — 4.5km loop, 2–3hrs, moderate. Start before 8am on weekends to have it to yourself. Empress Falls sprays — take a rain jacket.
  - **Bushrangers Cave & Walls Cave, Blackheath** — off Grand Canyon track; Walls Cave ceiling has ancient wave patterns; best at midday.
  - **Leura Cascades → Pool of Siloam** — 2km return, still green pool among cliffs; best after rain.
  - **Rocket Point** — spectacular panoramic lookout, flat easy 1km return, sunset is superb (western aspect).
  - **Minnehaha Falls, Katoomba** — 800m return, 10-min walk from town centre, gorgeous after rain; combine with dinner on Katoomba Street.
  - **Fairy Bower, Katoomba** — tiny grotto with seasonal waterfall, old-world charm, 500m detour off Prince Henry Cliff Walk.
  - **Den Fenella, Leura** — hidden cleft with seasonal waterfall into an amphitheatre; ~1.5km return; likely all to yourself.
  - **Popes Glen Track, Blackheath** — quiet fern-filled gully, cooler in summer.
  - **Mount Solitary via Ruined Castle** — 21km, 8–10hrs, hard — only if we want a big day.
  - General advice: check NSW National Parks conditions, carry water/sun protection/first aid.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 — Saturday 29 Aug: Strathfield → Blue Mountains (drive + afternoon/sunset shoot)
- Morning: depart Strathfield (drive ~1.5–2 hrs via Great Western Highway M4/A32).
- Aim to arrive mid-morning in the **Wentworth Falls / Leura / Katoomba** area to start early and beat weekend crowds.
- Morning: **Valley of the Waters Circuit** (Wentworth Falls) or the easier **Princes Rock** short walk — waterfalls + Jamison Valley.
- Lunch: Katoomba Street (many cafes) or picnic at Leura Cascades.
- Afternoon: checkout **Lincoln's Rock** (sunset, 2-min walk from car) and/or **Olympic Rock / Sublime Point** for Mount Solitary at golden hour.
- Evening: settle into BnB/hotel; optional dinner at Katoomba Street or Lilianfels' Darley's if splurging.

### Day 2 — Sunday 30 Aug: morning sunrise shoot → explore → drive back
- **Sunrise:** **Govetts Leap, Blackheath** (easy, drive to car park, only photographers around) or **Narrow Neck** near Katoomba.
- Morning walk: **Grand Canyon Loop, Blackheath** (start at Evans Lookout; 6km, ~3hrs, quiet with caves along the way) OR the easier **Cliff Top Walk Pulpit Rock → Govetts Leap** (4.5km one way, panoramic).
- Brunch: Blackheath café town (quiet, pretty).
- Afternoon: optional **Mount Hay Road** detour (Butterbox Point / Fortress Rock — only if road conditions allow) OR **scenic return via Bells Line of Road: Mount Tomah Botanic Garden** (see below).
- Afternoon/early evening: drive back to Strathfield.

### Alternative return via Bells Line of Road — Mount Tomah

Instead of going back the same way (Great Western Highway), take Bells Line of Road (B59) from Blackheath heading east. This is a quieter, windier, and far more scenic route through orchards and ridge-top farmland — a photographer's drive in itself. ~30 min from Blackheath you hit **Blue Mountains Botanic Garden Mount Tomah** — a cool-climate botanic garden perched at 1,000m with sweeping views to the east. In early September, spring bulbs and magnolias start blooming; the geometric formal gardens, specimen trees, and cloud-forest vibe make for great close-up and landscape photography. Free entry. Cafe on site (The Potager). The drive from Mount Tomah back to Strathfield is ~1.5 hrs via Richmond and the M7 — no longer than the normal route, just different.

### Backup / weather plan
- If rain is heavy, trail conditions can be slippery — prefer sealed-lookout drives (Echo Point, Govetts Leap, Hargraves, Lincoln's Rock) and skip steep stairs.
- Check [park alerts](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/visit-a-park/parks/blue-mountains-national-park/local-alerts) before departing and before each walk.

## Final Verdict (all four hotels) — based on genuine guest reviews

**Book Leisure Inn Spires — Studio @ $119 or One-Bedroom Apt @ $132 (Leura).** Recommended over Wilson's because it's **cheaper (~$34–47 less) and more central** for our route, while still covering cleanliness, safety, private bathroom, accessibility and location — with WiFi + free on-site parking.

- **Why Leisure Inn wins:**
  - **Location 9.4** — most central base (Leura village): ~5 min to Sublime Point/Olympic Rock/Leura Cascades, ~10 min to both Katoomba & Wentworth Falls, ~15–20 min to Blackheath. 2-min walk to Leura's restaurants/boutiques. 1,148 reviews (well-tested).
  - **Safety:** secure private parking under the units, quiet 3-acre gardens, night-box key pickup; ID + credit card at check-in.
  - **Cleanliness 8.5** — reviews: "large clean apartment... very quiet", "clean and a good size", "very clean and well equipped".
  - **Accessibility:** apartment-style with attached bathroom; one-bedroom adds kitchen + balcony. Ask for a ground-floor unit if needed.
  - **Private bathroom ✅ · Free WiFi ✅ · Free secure on-site parking ✅**
  - **Trade-offs:** WiFi 7.5 (adequate but not stellar), ~$35 damage deposit, ~1–3.5% credit-card surcharges, office-hours reception (Mon–Fri 9–5, weekends 10–4).
- **Why not Wilson's @ $166:** highest scores (cleanliness 9.9, location 9.6) and a superb ground-floor private studio — but it's **the most expensive, parking is off-site, and Wentworth Falls is ~20 min from Katoomba/Blackheath**, adding driving on both days.
- **Budget fallback — La Maison Deluxe Queen $107 (incl. breakfast):** cheapest with disabled-guest facilities and covered parking; but cleanliness 8.5 and WiFi 7.2, older building.
- **Avoid — Hotel Avonleigh Deluxe King $113:** superb WiFi (10) and value, but fails your **accessibility** priority (winding staircase, no lift).

> **Bottom line:** Book **Leisure Inn Spires @ $119–132** — the best cost-effective balance that is genuinely central to your photo route. Choose Wilson's only if you're willing to pay a premium for the absolute top cleanliness/safety scores.
