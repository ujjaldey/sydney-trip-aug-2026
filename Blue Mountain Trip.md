### Day 1 — Waterfalls & Lookouts (Wentworth Falls → Leura)

> **Drive:** Strathfield → Wentworth Falls → Leura → Katoomba (~2 hrs + stops)
> **Overnight:** Katoomba / Leura

### [Valley of the Waters / National Pass (Wentworth Falls)](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Valley+of+the+Waters+Wentworth+Falls+NSW)

![Wentworth Falls](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Upper_Wentworth_Falls_3,_NSW,_Australia_-_Nov_2008.jpg?width=400)\
[*📷 Google Images*](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Valley+of+the+Waters+Wentworth+Falls+NSW)

The best waterfall photography trail in the Blue Mountains. A 4.5 km loop descending through rainforest along the cliff edge, passing 5–8 waterfalls including the massive Empress Falls and the 100m Wentworth Falls themselves. After winter rain, the flow is at its peak — this is why late August is the best time of year for this trail.

- **Photo style:** Long-exposure cascades on moss-covered rocks, mist through the rainforest canopy, the grand Wentworth Falls drop from the bottom or the clifftop platform
- **Best time:** Morning (before 8am) — you'll have the falls almost entirely to yourself. The light filters through the canopy creating soft, even exposure
- **Walk:** 4.5 km loop · 2–3 hrs · Moderate · Many stairs
- **Access:** Park at the Wentworth Falls Picnic Area or Conservation Hut. Trailhead is well-signposted
- **Crowds:** Moderate by 10am on weekends — start early

> **Rain gear:** Empress Falls throws heavy spray. Bring a rain jacket for your camera even on dry days.

### [Lincoln's Rock (Wentworth Falls)](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Lincolns+Rock+Wentworth+Falls+NSW)

![Lincoln's Rock](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Three_Sisters_Sunset.jpg?width=400)\
[*📷 Google Images*](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Lincolns+Rock+Wentworth+Falls+NSW)

A flat, open rock platform suspended over the Jamison Valley — the single easiest epic landscape shot in the Blue Mountains. Walk 2 minutes from the car, stand on the edge (safely), and you're looking straight down the escarpment into the valley. The rock has a natural infinity-pool effect: a shallow depression collects rainwater and perfectly mirrors the sky and cliffs beyond.

- **Photo style:** Reflection shot of the escarpment in the rock pool, silhouettes at sunset, wide panorama of the Jamison Valley
- **Best time:** Golden hour / sunset — the western light paints the escarpment orange-pink
- **Walk:** 2 min from car park · Flat · Sealed path
- **Access:** Park at the end of Little Switzerland Drive, Wentworth Falls
- **Crowds:** Can get busy at sunset — arrive 30 min early to claim the best composition spot

### [Leura Cascades → Pool of Siloam](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Leura+Cascades+Blue+Mountains+NSW)

![Leura Cascades](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Upper_Wentworth_Falls_3,_NSW,_Australia_-_Nov_2008.jpg?width=400)\
[*📷 Google Images*](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Leura+Cascades+Blue+Mountains+NSW)

A quiet 2 km return track through fern-filled temperate rainforest. The cascades themselves are a series of small waterfalls and still pools — nothing as big as Wentworth Falls, but far more intimate and almost always empty. At the end, the Pool of Siloam is a deep-green natural pool enclosed by cliffs and ferns. Unreal stillness. After rain, the cascades swell and the greens are electric.

- **Photo style:** Intimate waterfall details, reflections in the still pool, fern-framed compositions, soft rainforest light
- **Best time:** Midday — the soft, filtered rainforest canopy light is ideal
- **Walk:** 2 km return · 45 min · Easy
- **Access:** Park at the Leura Cascades car park (off Cliff Drive). Well-signposted track
- **Crowds:** Very low — most people go to Echo Point instead

### [Sublime Point (Leura)](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Sublime+Point+Leura+Blue+Mountains)

![Sublime Point](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Three_Sisters_Sunset.jpg?width=400)\
[*📷 Google Images*](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Sublime+Point+Leura+Blue+Mountains)

A 5-minute walk from the car to a lookout over the Jamison Valley with Mount Solitary sitting dead centre. It's called Sublime for a reason. On misty mornings, the valley fills with fog and Mount Solitary floats above it like an island. Far fewer tourists than Echo Point or the Three Sisters — mostly locals with coffee cups at sunrise.

- **Photo style:** Mount Solitary emerging from the mist, valley fog at sunrise, golden-hour side-light on the escarpment. The rock platform here is wide and flat — great for tripod work
- **Best time:** Sunrise — the valley mist is strongest early, and you'll be alone with maybe one other photographer
- **Walk:** 5 min from car park · Sealed path + small stairs
- **Access:** Car park at the end of Sublime Point Road, Leura
- **Crowds:** Very low at sunrise — picks up by mid-morning

### Evening — Katoomba

After Sublime Point, drive 5 min into Katoomba. Dinner on Katoomba Street — **Echoes Restaurant** (fancy, Jamison Valley views) or **The Yellow Deli** (cosy, unique, closes early). If the sky is clear, a quick night shoot at the lit-up Three Sisters from Echo Point is a low-effort bonus — arrive after 9pm and it'll be empty.

### Day 2 — Canyons & Valley Vistas (Blackheath → Mount Tomah)

> **Drive:** Katoomba → Blackheath → Bells Line of Road → Mount Tomah → Strathfield (~3 hrs + stops)

### [Govetts Leap (Blackheath)](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Govetts+Leap+Blackheath+Blue+Mountains)

![Blackheath](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/2023-05-13_Blackheath,_New_South_Wales.jpg?width=400)\
[*📷 Google Images*](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Govetts+Leap+Blackheath+Blue+Mountains)

Arguably the best sunrise in the Blue Mountains. A massive cliff-edge lookout over the Grose Valley with Bridal Veil Falls plunging 180m off the opposite wall. Drive straight to the car park — zero walking required. At sunrise, the first light hits the far cliffs and the valley glows gold. The only other people here before 7am are photographers.

- **Photo style:** Wide-angle of the Grose Valley at sunrise, Bridal Veil Falls in the distance with golden cliff light, mist filling the valley floor
- **Best time:** Sunrise — non-negotiable for this spot. Arrive 30 min before sunrise to set up
- **Walk:** 0 min — the lookout is at the car park
- **Access:** Govetts Leap Road, Blackheath. Large sealed car park
- **Crowds:** Only photographers at sunrise; busy by 10am

### [Grand Canyon Loop (Blackheath)](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Grand+Canyon+track+Blackheath+Blue+Mountains)

![Grand Canyon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/2023-05-13_Blackheath,_New_South_Wales.jpg?width=400)\
[*📷 Google Images*](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Grand+Canyon+track+Blackheath+Blue+Mountains)

The best walk in the Blue Mountains, and surprisingly quiet. A 6 km circuit starting from Evans Lookout (or Neates Glen) descending into a narrow, fern-lined canyon with sandstone walls, small waterfalls, a creek crossing, and caves along the track. It feels like a different world — cool, damp, and completely enclosed by the canyon walls. The variety — canyon floor, fern gullies, cliff-top lookouts — makes it the richest photographic walk on this trip.

- **Photo style:** Canyon walls closing in, ferns and moss glowing in filtered light, cave interiors, the cliff-top view from Evans Lookout looking back into the Grose Valley
- **Best time:** Morning — the canyon is east-facing and gets good indirect light early. Start by 8am for the best conditions
- **Walk:** 6 km loop · 2.5–3 hrs · Moderate · Some steep sections and stairs
- **Access:** Park at Evans Lookout, Blackheath. Walk starts from there (clockwise recommended: Evans Lookout → Neates Glen → return)
- **Crowds:** Low — the Grade 4 rating scares off casual walkers

### [Pulpit Rock Lookout (Blackheath)](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Pulpit+Rock+Blackheath+Blue+Mountains)

![Pulpit Rock](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/2023-05-13_Blackheath,_New_South_Wales.jpg?width=400)\
[*📷 Google Images*](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Pulpit+Rock+Blackheath+Blue+Mountains)

A short walk off the Cliff Top Track between Govetts Leap and Evans Lookout. Pulpit Rock is a narrow sandstone pinnacle jutting out into the Grose Valley — 280° views, completely isolated, and feels like you're standing on the nose of a ship floating above the valley. If the Grand Canyon walk is too long or conditions aren't favourable, this is the perfect alternative: a 1 km detour from the Govetts Leap car park, or accessible as part of the 4.5 km Cliff Top Walk.

- **Photo style:** Standing on the pinnacle (or shooting it from the side), wide panorama of the Grose Valley, the track itself — narrow cliff-edge path with dramatic drop-offs
- **Best time:** Late morning / early afternoon — the valley is lit from above
- **Walk:** 1 km return from the nearest track junction; 4.5 km one-way if doing the full Cliff Top Walk
- **Access:** From Evans Lookout car park or Govetts Leap car park; follow the Cliff Top Track signs
- **Crowds:** Very low — most people stop at the main lookouts

### [Blue Mountains Botanic Garden — Mount Tomah](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Mount+Tomah+Botanic+Garden+Blue+Mountains) *(return via Bells Line of Road)*

![Mount Tomah](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Australia_-_Part_3_(27446932621).jpg?width=400)\
[*📷 Google Images*](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Mount+Tomah+Botanic+Garden+Blue+Mountains)

Instead of going back the same way, take Bells Line of Road (B59) east from Blackheath — a quieter, far more scenic ridge-top drive through orchards and farmland. After about 30 minutes you reach Mount Tomah Botanic Garden at 1,000m elevation. In early September, the cool-climate gardens are waking up — spring bulbs, magnolia blossoms, and the formal geometric garden beds set against a sweeping eastern vista. Free entry. Great café (The Potager) for a final coffee before the drive home.

- **Photo style:** Formal garden geometry against wild mountain backdrop, macro of spring blooms, cloud-forest atmosphere in the cool-climate sections, the view from the top terrace looking east
- **Best time:** Afternoon — soft light on the gardens, quietest time of day
- **Walk:** Paved paths throughout · Wheelchair accessible · 1–2 hrs to explore
- **Access:** Bells Line of Road, Mount Tomah. Free entry. Cafe on site.
- **Drive to Strathfield:** ~1.5 hrs via Richmond and the M7

---

> **Backup / weather plan:** If rain is heavy, trail conditions can be slippery — swap the Grand Canyon walk for the easier Pulpit Rock detour, and skip the Valley of the Waters stairs. Sealed lookouts (Govetts Leap, Sublime Point, Lincoln's Rock) are fine in any weather. Check [park alerts](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/visit-a-park/parks/blue-mountains-national-park/local-alerts) before departing.

## Final Verdict (all four hotels) — based on genuine guest reviews

**Book Leisure Inn Spires — Studio @ $119 or One-Bedroom Apt @ $132 (Leura).** Recommended over Wilson's because it's **cheaper (~$34–47 less) and more central** for our route, while still covering cleanliness, safety, private bathroom, accessibility and location — with WiFi + free on-site parking.

- **Why Leisure Inn wins:**
  - **Location 9.4** — most central base (Leura village): ~5 min to Sublime Point/Olympic Rock/Leura Cascades, ~10 min to both Katoomba & Wentworth Falls, ~15–20 min to Blackheath. 2-min walk to Leura's restaurants/boutiques. 1,148 reviews (well-tested).
  - **Safety:** secure private parking under the units, quiet 3-acre gardens, night-box key pickup; ID + credit card at check-in.
  - **Cleanliness 8.5** — reviews: "large clean apartment... very quiet", "clean and a good size", "very clean and well equipped".
  - **Accessibility:** apartment-style with attached bathroom; one-bedroom adds kitchen + balcony. Ask for a ground-floor unit if needed.
  - **Private bathroom ✅ · Free WiFi ✅ · Free secure on-site parking ✅**
  - **Trade-offs:** WiFi 7.5 (adequate but not stellar), ~$35 damage deposit, ~1–3.5% credit-card surcharges, office-hours reception (Mon–Fri 9–5, weekends 10–4).
- **Why not Wilson's @ $166:** highest scores (cleanliness 9.9, location 9.6) and a superb ground-floor private studio — but it's **the most expensive, parking is off-site, and Wentworth Falls is ~20 min from Katoomba/Blackheath**, adding driving on both days.
- **Budget fallback — La Maison Deluxe Queen $107 (incl. breakfast):** cheapest with disabled-guest facilities and covered parking; but cleanliness 8.5 and WiFi 7.2, older building.
- **Avoid — Hotel Avonleigh Deluxe King $113:** superb WiFi (10) and value, but fails your **accessibility** priority (winding staircase, no lift).

> **Bottom line:** Book **Leisure Inn Spires @ $119–132** — the best cost-effective balance that is genuinely central to your photo route. Choose Wilson's only if you're willing to pay a premium for the absolute top cleanliness/safety scores.

> **Key light check:** Govetts Leap sunrise is non-negotiable — the valley faces east and the first light on the cliffs is the money shot. Sublime Point for mist requires cold, clear nights followed by still mornings. Valley of the Waters is best 2–3 days after heavy rain when the flow is strong but the track has dried out.

## References & Further Reading

*Blogs from recent years (roughly 2022–2026) by photographers and nature lovers who avoid the crowds. Priority is given to landscape photographers and quiet-walks specialists.*

### 1. Gary P Hayes — Landscape Photographer (Blue Mountains local)
- **Blog:** [A Landscape Photographer's Guide to Australia's Blue Mountains](https://garyphayes.photography/a-landscape-photographers-guide-to-visiting-the-blue-mountains-australia/) + [Gary's Photo Hotspots](https://garyphayes.photography/garys-photo-guide/)
- **Why relevant:** Gary is a full-time landscape photographer who *lives* in Mount Victoria. His guide is written specifically for visiting photographers. Has a free 9MB PDF version of the guide — worth downloading.
- **Key takeaways:**
  - Breaks the upper mountains into 7 "photographic zones" and advises basing yourself near the light direction you want (west/east-facing lookouts are best for golden hour).
  - Best ~1–3 hr photographic trails: **National Pass loop** (Wentworth Falls, 5–8 waterfalls), **Leura Cascades loop**, **Furber Steps** (Katoomba), **Grand Canyon** (Blackheath), **Rigby Hill / Walls Lookout** (Bells Line, quiet).
  - Best sunrise/sunset spots: Govett's Leap sunrise (Blackheath), Narrow Neck sunrise (Katoomba), Hanging Rock (Blackheath), Hornes Point sunset (Mount Victoria), Mount Banks sunset.
  - Iconic shots: Sylvia Falls, Mount Solitary from Olympic Rock / Sublime Point, Three Sisters at sunrise from Eaglehawk Lookout.
  - Winter (Jun–Aug) = big rains and his best waterfall shots; dramatic skies at sunset, less at sunrise. Snow is rare but possible.
  - Drive from Sydney CBD ~90 min; car essential for photographers.

### 2. Walk My World — Travel Couple (hiking + lookouts)
- **Blog:** [28 Blue Mountains Lookouts including hidden gems](https://www.walkmyworld.com/posts/most-beautiful-lookouts-in-the-blue-mountains) (Nov 2022)
- **Why relevant:** A couple who hike constantly and actively seek out quiet, under-the-radar lookouts — very close to our style.
- **Key takeaways — quiet lookouts (rarely busy):**
  - **Fortress Rock, Leura** — rarely visited, view into Grose Valley, easy ~2.8km walk.
  - **Lockleys Pylon, Leura** — very quiet even midday; ~7km return; access road rough (4WD advised).
  - **Du Faur Head, Leura** — "hardly anyone knows about it"; effort to reach, worth it.
  - **Butterbox Point, Leura** — unknown spot, dramatic, ~3km return; end of Mount Hay Road (unsealed).
  - **Castle Head, Katoomba** — quiet, peaceful, views of Ruined Castle & Mount Solitary.
  - **Olympian Rock, Leura** — "not another soul in sight" on a Saturday midday.
  - **Rigby Hill & Walls Lookout, Bilpin/Mount Wilson** — rarely visited, unrailed, wild, great sunset.
  - **Kanangra Walls / Plateau** — solitude and phenomenal views (distant, needs ~2hr drive on rough road).
- **Key takeaways — accessible & iconic:**
  - **Lincoln's Rock, Wentworth Falls** — their favourite; 2-min walk; huge space even when busy; best at sunset.
  - **Echo Point, Katoomba** — go at sunrise (or at night — lit up, no crowds).
  - **Govetts Leap, Blackheath** — sunrise, only the odd photographer; busy later in day.
  - **Hargraves Lookout, Blackheath** — golden hour over Megalong Valley.
- **Accommodation notes:** Lilianfels (luxury, metres from Three Sisters, fine dining Darley's), Katoomba YHA (budget, private rooms). Full guide: [where to stay in the Blue Mountains](https://www.walkmyworld.com/posts/blue-mountains-accommodation-hotels).

### 3. NSW National Parks & Wildlife Service (official, updated Apr 2026)
- **Blog:** [Blue Mountains spots that give the must-sees a run for their money](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/get-inspired/stories/blue-mountains-spots-that-give-the-must-sees-a-run-for-their-money)
- **Why relevant:** Official source for quieter alternatives to the icons; park alerts & current conditions.
- **Key takeaways:**
  - **Sublime Point, Leura** — morning to catch Mount Solitary emerging from mist.
  - **Mount Hay Road, Leura** — epic vistas, "probably have it all to yourself", drive or mountain bike.
  - **National Pass + Overcliff–Undercliff Track, Wentworth Falls** — cliff-edge views, swimming holes; finish with coffee at Conservation Hut.
  - **Pulpit Rock lookout + Pulpit walking track from Govetts Leap, Blackheath** — isolated pinnacle, secret picnic, 280° views.
  - **Portal lookout & Blue Pool, Glenbrook** — swimming below epic green backdrop.
  - **Weather (early Aug):** cold — min ~2°C, max ~13–15°C. Always check [park alerts](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/visit-a-park/parks/blue-mountains-national-park/local-alerts).

### 4. My Katoomba — Local Cottage Hosts (Katoomba locals)
- **Blog:** [10 Hidden Walks in the Blue Mountains Most Tourists Never Find](https://www.mykatoomba.com/blog/hidden-walks-blue-mountains) (Feb 2026)
- **Why relevant:** Written by people who live and host in Katoomba; exactly the "quiet trails locals keep" angle.
- **Key takeaways:**
  - **Valley of the Waters Circuit, Wentworth Falls** — 4.5km loop, 2–3hrs, moderate. Start before 8am on weekends to have it to yourself. Empress Falls sprays — take a rain jacket.
  - **Bushrangers Cave & Walls Cave, Blackheath** — off Grand Canyon track; Walls Cave ceiling has ancient wave patterns; best at midday.
  - **Leura Cascades → Pool of Siloam** — 2km return, still green pool among cliffs; best after rain.
  - **Rocket Point** — spectacular panoramic lookout, flat easy 1km return, sunset is superb (western aspect).
  - **Minnehaha Falls, Katoomba** — 800m return, 10-min walk from town centre, gorgeous after rain; combine with dinner on Katoomba Street.
  - **Fairy Bower, Katoomba** — tiny grotto with seasonal waterfall, old-world charm, 500m detour off Prince Henry Cliff Walk.
  - **Den Fenella, Leura** — hidden cleft with seasonal waterfall into an amphitheatre; ~1.5km return; likely all to yourself.
  - **Popes Glen Track, Blackheath** — quiet fern-filled gully, cooler in summer.
  - **Mount Solitary via Ruined Castle** — 21km, 8–10hrs, hard — only if we want a big day.
  - General advice: check NSW National Parks conditions, carry water/sun protection/first aid.
